# Yunethre environment and settlement foundation

Yunethre retains its 27 authored plains hexes between the South Oremindi and West Lotharn Mountains. Region ID 38 is appended to the desktop registry. Registration preserves the former unbuilt ground contribution to neighbouring terrain; the new low grassland relief and settlement grading act only on Yunethre's own cells.

The western Lakeside Free Town stands immediately east of the existing Forest Tarn. This is a descriptive working label, not an invented character or political power. It is independent of the Ambroni Empire, Celder and Elfland. Human timber-and-lime buildings, elven high-eaved homes and a broad open centaur shelter share an unwalled market common. Its six exterior buildings contain no authored interiors or invented named civilians. The shoreline and 35-metre lake surface remain owned by South Oremindi. A three-part timber promenade uses physical ramp/deck surfaces to connect the lower town to the lake without changing either region's terrain or pretending the lake lies at sea level.

Bane's Camp stands on the eastern half of the passage, separate from the neutral town. Seven broad canvas tents, three supply wagons, packed-earth common ground, targets and recurved bow racks establish a mobile equestrian society. Wagons are supply trailers; there are no horse pens or invented livestock. Its open centre and western exit are reserved for centaur bodies. The tested raiding approach winds south through open Yunethre ground into northwestern Isareos; it avoids both the neutral town and Elfland and follows dry, manageable ground around the existing beck banks.

Low shoulders, tawny grass, scattered flowers, stone fragments and irregular sheltered groves provide variety while retaining broad open plains. Every visible tree has a registered harvestable species: white oak, silver birch or hawthorn. Own random streams and spatial instance batches preserve other regions' scenery and avoid one draw call per plant.

Twenty-five permanent non-settlement habitats support 50 deer, boar and upland hares. The town and camp cells remain clear. Wildlife uses the existing resident controller, retaining identities, testing body clearance and slope, and staying within its region when roaming or fleeing. Culling never recreates the population.

This foundation leaves human, elf and centaur civilian characterization, trade, camp life, faction permissions and the wider centaur campaign for later design. The political premise is survival after human expansion, with Elfland trading with and supporting the centaurs. No new named civilians or hats were introduced by this environment work.

Validation: all eight focused Yunethre checks passed, including actual creation of 50 resident animals, grounded movement for every represented species, culling and revisiting, typed trees, the dry raiding circuit and connected promenade support. The four-check combined-world integration pass also passed: main-road ordering, settlement entrances, Menora bridges, Caricas routes and accessible farm benches, and Yunethre tracks, centaur clearance and the entire lakeside ramp. That integration run preceded the final Caricas farm relocations; it does not claim to validate those later coordinates.
