# Nether Desert environment

The 26 authored atlas hexes now form a modest stony plateau above the upper Neth: broad low rock backs, gravel exposures, two interrupted dry washes, three shallow rain pans and sparse drought scrub. This follows the region’s lore: quiet aridity, without the great dunes or canyons of the Meroshe deserts. There are no new people, buildings, ruins, farms or permanent waterholes.

The interior reaches roughly 30–40 metres. All relief is part of the actual terrain height field; there are no invisible mountain collision boxes. The change fades across 74 metres at the region boundary and leaves the existing Neth channel and banks intact. Four natural cross-country walk corridors connect the arrival to the landmarks without adding artificial roads. Existing neighbouring terrain remains responsible for the outermost boundary shape.

Low wormwood and rockrose concentrate beside the washes. Bare exposures have flattened stone chips and fractured slabs; the rain pans show pale dry silt and fine cracks. Props use the rendered terrain height so their roots and bases meet the visible ground. Only the few substantial visible slabs have small collision cores. Each hex is a merged, independently culled scenery batch, with cooperative construction steps for Fast loading.

Sparse terrestrial wildlife includes basking spine lizards, upland hares and solitary canyon tortoises, with one bone-bird overhead. These are explicit habitat extensions of existing Azhora fauna, not claims that the Nether Desert lore names endemic animals. Browsers live beside wash vegetation; lizards live on dry rock. Persistent sites are checked against actual owned terrain and gentle slopes. No livestock or human owners are introduced.

Testing travel uses `NETHER_DESERT_ARRIVAL` at **(-2844, 694)**. Review views are `nether-desert`, `nether-desert-pan` and `nether-desert-wash`. Focused coverage in `tests/nether-desert-world.test.js` checks atlas ownership, continuous boundary contribution, river preservation, walkable interior and landmark routes, habitat placement, grounded scenery, visible collision and mesh budgets. Native integration and final screenshot review are handled with the main world build.

Landscape screenshots were inspected in the actual Electron renderer. All named natural landmarks are discoverable on the journal map and available under F8 / Go anywhere. Focused region tests cover terrain continuity, grounded vegetation, traversable corridors and wildlife habitat.
