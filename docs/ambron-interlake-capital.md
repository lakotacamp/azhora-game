# Ambron: interlake capital

The capital now occupies dry land between Ela, the Thelas chain, Brul and Ossen. Its center is (-1130, 10). The irregular walled footprint is 54,290 square metres, more than twice the former rectangular settlement. The lakes, Link and Ela-south retain their existing channels and shorelines.

The north contains the tall Seat, temple, records and assembly tower. A narrow market spine leads into a broad southern district of workshops, guild courts and residential lanes. Forty-six varied building plots use the existing four-period masonry kit. Existing residents keep their identities; no additional civilians were invented.

Five working gates connect the Plain, Ossen, Brul, Lake and Raft approaches. Streets, collision boundaries and autopilot enclosures share the same outline. Graded ground follows that outline with a short berm and feather, leaving the concave lake shoulders natural.

Cagney waits beside three modest roadside cottages at (-922, 194). Her three persistent, camouflaged gangs occupy the outside approach; the walk inside the city is quiet. Her house and Ben's face the same lane. Troy remains in a separate working quarter. Existing house IDs, mailboxes, rewards and knock behavior are retained. Jesse's carriage route ends at the Carpenter's Guild's north frontage, with separate cart parking and a named mailbox.
