# Ibenwood grove implementation trial

30 September 2026. The user has authorized **one representative grove and its surrounding forest**, followed by assessment of usage, elapsed time, visual quality, and corrections. The wider five-region design remains in [the design draft](ibenwood-design-draft.md). Dwarfland is a separate design-only follow-up.

## Scope

Build a representative environment that can be reached and examined from the desktop app. Use one small inhabited-looking elven grove with a substantial forest approach. Include dwellings among branches, homes around enormous roots, and ancient stone buildings partly absorbed into the forest. This is an ordinary grove, not the royal heart. No new named civilian cast or quests are required.

The first pass evaluates environment construction and integration. Full ranger perception, territorial combat, permission quests, dimensional withdrawal, and the remaining groves are outside this pilot. Do not label all Ibenwood complete or invent those undecided systems to fill the scene.

## Required results

- The grove has a deliberate ground-level arrival and distinguishable silhouettes in the game's low-poly style. Its three building forms coexist rather than being three disconnected samples.
- Surrounding forest includes giant veterans, smaller trees, saplings in light gaps, fallen wood, roots, ferns, and sheltered clearings. Preserve walkable passages and camera readability.
- Each living tree has a concrete species. Use existing Ibenwood flora names where appropriate, with recognizable forms. Trees meet the rendered ground. Gathering fallen wood is permitted; living-tree felling is prohibited here with an understandable explanation. Do not invent a punishment system.
- Use persistent wildlife across the surroundings, including ground animals as well as birds. Reuse suitable existing rigs and behavior. Ordinary fauna dominates; subtle unusual flora can suggest the interior.
- Trunks, buildings, and substantial rocks have suitable collision. Main walking approaches and views remain accessible. Elevated dwellings may be exterior-only in this pilot; avoid promising a complete canopy traversal system.
- Provide a clearly labeled testing-tools destination and reproducible desktop views. Preserve normal saves. Any partial regional registration must be described honestly as a pilot, with a location on the actual atlas and no arbitrary reassignment of neighboring territory.
- Prefer a focused module and existing systems. Preserve line endings and unrelated changes. Do not edit World Builder or integrate unrelated worktrees. Region IDs 28-31 are reserved for separate Mithala work; avoid collisions if registration is needed.

## Review and measurement

Record the exact worker model, start and finish times, files changed, checks run, and issues found in review. Separate the implementation worker's elapsed time from orchestration and review. Record exact token or credit usage only if the tools expose it; otherwise mark it unavailable. This is a single implementation trial, not a controlled comparison or evidence of a percentage saving against Astra.

Check the real scene at player height and from a wider view. Review tree grounding, approach traversal, building readability, wildlife presence, collision, and render cost. Run focused automated checks plus a desktop smoke/capture using disposable test state. Record the number and nature of substantive correction rounds. Do not claim visual quality from code inspection alone.

The final report should explain how to reach the grove, show or link the checked views, state what is implemented, identify remaining limitations, and assess whether the worker produced a useful foundation with an acceptable amount of correction.
