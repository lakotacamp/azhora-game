# West Lotharn integration and refinement

29 September 2026. This supersedes the implementation details in the [original draft report](west-lotharn-report.md); the [brief](west-lotharn-brief.md) remains the scope reference.

## Main build

Claude's West Lotharn draft (`1722740`, originally based on `8cc891b`) is integrated with the later Drent household and species-aware tree work on main. West Lotharn is region 27. Its terrain, scenery, water, map discoveries, regional sky, wildlife, developer travel, climbing and caves use the main game's systems. The regional survey was regenerated with its normal generator.

In the desktop app, open **F8 > Go anywhere > West Lotharn Mountains**. The default arrival is in the Long Valley. The developer bat and dragon retain their 900 m flight ceiling, above the approximately 550 m crest; landing still requires safe ground. Restart a running app to load the integrated files.

The region retains seven summits, the two main valleys, the eastern col, four watercourses and nine empty caves. This pass adds no residents, settlements, quests, roads or mines. It does not import the separate Mithala work in progress.

## Refinements

- Summit caps now blend continuously across neighboring masses. The original nearest-summit selection could produce a roughly 184 m height jump across a tiny boundary.
- Broad unequal buttresses and a narrower, sloping crown give the highest massif a less uniform silhouette. Upper cliff courses erode toward continuous slopes while the lower range keeps its distinctive layered cliffs and forest shelves.
- The forest now follows the actual rendered ground, including the fine mountain terrain triangles. Each trunk's footprint is checked; unsupported steep placements are rejected. Irregular groves, gaps, saplings, scrub and scree break up the repeated tree bands.
- All seven woodland species join the existing tree registry, so woodcutting removes both the visible tree and its collision and retains the game's saved harvest/regrowth behavior.
- Trail joins and crossfalls are evaluated using actual movement and stamina, in addition to the geometric route tests. The full crest ascent regression follows the authored trail with ordinary collision, falling and climbing, without teleporting or unlimited stamina.
- A four-metre rest shelf near the final crest ascent breaks up the exposed climb. Its height is recovered gently on the next ledge, preserving the summit and avoiding a stamina increase. The last ramp uses a steady grade instead of an eased profile that briefly crossed the walking limit.
- Small trail irregularities adding up to 8 cm above the normal walking grade can be stepped over only when the complete face is within the walking slope limit. This closes a movement dead zone where a tiny bump was too steep to walk across but too shallow to grab. Cliffs and diagonal steep faces still require climbing.
- Cave portals use the final terrain profile. Both Lotharn regions share the same cave controller; a checkpoint made underground recovers at that cave's safe entrance.
- Map descriptions describe the place itself rather than exposing atlas or implementation details.

The final forest has 3,153 trees across seven species. The region also registers 24 persistent wildlife actors, four watercourses and nine caves. Tree grounding is checked against the actual summit and fine cave-mouth triangles, not the deliberately sunk coarse terrain underneath.

Visual captures are generated by:

```sh
node scripts/launch.cjs --smoke-test --review-clean --review-jpeg --review-views=west-lotharn-crest,west-lotharn-valley,west-lotharn-ramp
```

The checked views show the crown, wooded valley and ascent shelves; the renderer reported no frame errors. Images are local review artifacts under `tests/artifacts/`.
